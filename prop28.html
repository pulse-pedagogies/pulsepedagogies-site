Proposition 28 research

Our research on California's Prop 28 Arts and Music in Schools program. For practical guidance, including the 2026 changes from AB 2440, see the clearAMS Prop 28 guide.

Proposition 28, the Arts and Music in Schools Funding Guarantee and Accountability Act, guarantees nearly $1 billion a year for arts education in California, along with compliance, staffing and transparency requirements that most district systems were not built to manage.
