Security and compliance at Pulse Pedagogies

How we build: a layered architecture with no client-side AI keys, a zero-student-data posture in clearAMS, accessibility from the first screen, and privacy by design.

Every Pulse Pedagogies product is built on the same three-layer architecture, with no direct client-side exposure to any AI service or credential. It is designed for regulated environments such as education and government, with COPPA, FERPA and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility treated as design constraints from the start.
