Pulse Pedagogies: software for education, built by educators

Pulse Pedagogies, LLC is a software studio in Glendale, California, founded by two career educators. We design and build AI-powered web and mobile applications for schools, school districts, county offices of education and the people who run them.

In production

  • clearAMS: free Prop 28 Arts and Music in Schools planning and compliance for every California district, ready for AB 2440 site certification.
  • VAPA Pulse: arts curriculum support for visual and performing arts teachers, aligned to California's VAPA content standards and the National Core Arts Standards.
  • Signet: professional learning from the first request for a seat to a signed credential anyone can verify, inside the Google Workspace your organization already runs.

Read about our products in detail.

In development

  • FieldNote, for special education teams: AI-powered observation notes from a single photo, ready for IEP workflows.
  • Meridian, for school counselors: culturally responsive counseling action plans grounded in ASCA, CASEL and MTSS frameworks.
  • AdjunctCentral, for adjunct professors: pay tracking, timesheets and tax reporting across multiple institutions.
  • Vitae, for college faculty: a real-time CV builder that writes the annual review narrative.
  • CPQ, for administrators and program directors: categorical funding eligibility determinations with built-in audit documentation.

What we do

Product strategy, UI and UX design, design systems, web and mobile engineering, and AI-powered experiences, built on a compliance-first architecture designed around COPPA, FERPA and WCAG 2.1 AA. Read our security and compliance posture, or see our Prop 28 research.
